# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces the **Unified Kill Chain (UKC)**, a modern attack framework that combines multiple cyber kill chain models to better represent today's complex cyber attacks and help defenders identify opportunities to detect and stop attackers.

The room focuses on:
- Unified Kill Chain framework
- Threat modeling
- Multi-stage cyber attacks
- Attack propagation
- Defensive opportunities

- Understand the Unified Kill Chain
- Learn the phases of modern cyber attacks

- Improve defensive detection strategies

# 📌 Task 1 — Introduction

Learned how the Unified Kill Chain expands traditional attack models by covering the complete lifecycle of modern cyber attacks.

---

# ⛓️ Task 2 — What is a Kill Chain?

A Kill Chain is a framework that breaks an attack into multiple stages, allowing defenders to detect and disrupt attackers before they achieve their objectives.

---

# 🛡️ Task 3 — What is Threat Modelling?

Threat modeling helps organizations:

- Identify potential threats
- Understand attack paths
- Prioritize security controls
- Reduce organizational risk

It enables proactive defense rather than reactive response.

---

# 🔗 Task 4 — Introducing the Unified Kill Chain

Unlike the traditional Cyber Kill Chain, the Unified Kill Chain covers the entire attack lifecycle and includes:

- Initial intrusion
- Internal network propagation
- Final attacker objectives

This provides a more complete view of modern adversary operations.

---

# 🎯 Task 5 — Goal: In (Initial Foothold)

The attacker gains initial access through techniques such as:

- Phishing
- Exploiting vulnerabilities
- Stolen credentials
- Malicious attachments

The objective is to establish an initial foothold inside the target environment.

---

# 🌐 Task 6 — Goal: Through (Network Propagation)

After gaining access, attackers move throughout the environment by:

- Privilege escalation
- Lateral movement
- Credential dumping
- Internal reconnaissance
- Persistence

This stage focuses on expanding control within the network.

---

# 🎯 Task 7 — Goal: Out (Action on Objectives)

Once attackers reach valuable assets, they perform their final objectives, including:

- Data exfiltration
- Ransomware deployment
- Service disruption
- Financial theft
- System destruction
