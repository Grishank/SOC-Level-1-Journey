# 🧠 Room Overview

**Linux Threat Detection 2** focuses on attacker behavior after compromising a Linux system. The room explores the **Discovery** phase of the MITRE ATT&CK framework, attacker motivations, and the early actions performed after gaining access. SOC analysts learn how to detect reconnaissance activities, identify suspicious commands, and recognize indicators of compromise through Linux logs and system monitoring.

---

- Understand attacker discovery techniques
- Detect reconnaissance activity on Linux
- Analyze attacker motivations
- Investigate post-compromise actions
- Monitor suspicious Linux commands
