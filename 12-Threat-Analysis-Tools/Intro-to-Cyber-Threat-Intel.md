# 🧠 Room Overview

**Intro to Cyber Threat Intel** introduces the fundamentals of **Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** and explains how organizations collect, analyze, and share intelligence about cyber threats. The room covers the CTI lifecycle, intelligence types, industry frameworks, and standards that help security teams make informed decisions against evolving threats.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)
- Learn the CTI lifecycle
- Explore intelligence types
- Understand CTI standards and frameworks
- Learn how threat intelligence supports SOC operations
- Practice basic threat intelligence analysis
