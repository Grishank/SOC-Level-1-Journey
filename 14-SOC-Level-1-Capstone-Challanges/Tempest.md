# 🧠 Room Overview

**Tempest** is a comprehensive SOC Level 1 capstone challenge that simulates a complete cyber attack against a Windows workstation. As a SOC Analyst, you investigate every stage of the attack chain—from the initial phishing email to privilege escalation and full system compromise—using logs, forensic artifacts, and endpoint telemetry.

The room combines everything learned throughout the SOC Level 1 path into a realistic end-to-end incident investigation.

# 💡 SOC Investigation Tips

- Always build a timeline before drawing conclusions.
- Correlate endpoint, network, and authentication logs.
- Track parent-child process relationships carefully.
- Validate Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) before escalating.
- Analyze every phase of the attack chain—not just the initial alert.
