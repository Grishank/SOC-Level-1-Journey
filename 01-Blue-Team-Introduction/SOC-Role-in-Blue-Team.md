# 🧠 Room Overview

This room explains how a **Security Operations Center (SOC)** is structured and how different blue team roles work together to defend organizations against cyber threats.

The room focuses on:
- SOC hierarchy
- Blue team roles
- Analyst responsibilities
- Incident escalation
- Career progression in SOC environments

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand SOC team structure
- Learn the responsibilities of blue team members
- Understand SOC analyst levels (L1, L2, L3)
- Learn incident escalation workflows
- Explore cybersecurity career growth

---

# 👨‍💻 Task 1 — Introduction

Learned about the importance of blue team operations and how SOC analysts help organizations monitor and respond to security threats.

---

# 🏢 Task 2 — Security Hierarchy

Understood the different levels inside a SOC:

| Level | Role |
|---|---|
| L1 | Alert monitoring & triage |
| L2 | Deep investigation & response |
| L3 | Advanced threat hunting & malware analysis |

Other important roles:
- Security Engineers
- Threat Hunters
- DFIR Teams
- SOC Managers

---

# 🛡️ Task 3 — Meet the Blue Team

Blue team members work together to:
- Monitor alerts
- Analyze suspicious activity
- Investigate incidents
- Respond to threats
- Improve organizational security

---

# 📈 Task 4 — Advancing SOC Career

Typical SOC career progression:

```text
SOC Analyst L1 → SOC Analyst L2 → SOC Analyst L3 → Threat Hunter → Security Engineer → SOC Manager
```

Important skills for growth:
- Log analysis
- SIEM investigation
- Threat hunting
- Incident response
- Communication & documentation
