# 🧠 Room Overview

**Phishing Analysis Fundamentals** introduces the core concepts behind phishing emails and teaches how SOC analysts inspect emails to determine whether they are legitimate or malicious.

The room covers every major component of an email—from sender addresses to message headers—and explains how attackers abuse them during phishing campaigns.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand how email communication works
- Analyze sender email addresses
- Learn the email delivery process
- Read and interpret email headers
- Inspect phishing email bodies
- Identify different phishing attack types

---

# 📌 Task 1 — Introduction

Learned the importance of phishing analysis within Security Operations Centers (SOCs) and why email remains one of the most common attack vectors.

---

# 📌 Task 2 — The Email Address

Studied the structure of an email address, including:

- Local part (username)
- Domain name
- Mail domain validation
- Common spoofing techniques
- Look-alike domains (Typosquatting)

---

# 📌 Task 3 — Email Delivery

Learned how emails travel across the Internet through:

- SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol)
- Mail Transfer Agents (MTA)
- Mail Servers
- DNS Mail Exchange (MX) Records

Also explored why SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are important for email authentication.

---

# 📌 Task 4 — Email Headers

Examined important email header fields such as:

- From
- To
- Reply-To
- Return-Path
- Received
- Message-ID
- Date
- Authentication Results

Learned how headers help determine whether an email is genuine or spoofed.

---

# 📌 Task 5 — Email Body

Analyzed the content of phishing emails by identifying:

- Suspicious wording
- Urgent language
- Fake login pages
- Malicious links
- Dangerous attachments
- Social engineering techniques

---

# 📌 Task 6 — Types of Phishing

Explored different phishing attack categories including:

- Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Clone Phishing
- Smishing (SMS Phishing)
- Vishing (Voice Phishing)

Learned how attackers customize campaigns depending on the target.

---

# 🛡️ Defensive Skills Learned

- Email verification
- Header analysis
- Sender validation
- Link inspection
- Attachment awareness
- Domain reputation checking
- Email authentication concepts (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

# 🔑 Key Concepts

- SMTP
- Email Headers
- Email Authentication
- SPF
- DKIM

- MX Records
- Social Engineering
- Phishing Detection

---

# 💬 Key Takeaway

> "Most phishing emails reveal themselves through small indicators—careful analysis of headers, domains, authentication records, and message content allows defenders to identify malicious emails before users become victims."
